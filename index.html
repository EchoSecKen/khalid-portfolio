About // 01

I'm a medical doctor who fell into cybersecurity around my fourth year of med school, and never really climbed back out. It started as something to unwind with — a fun hobby on the side of a demanding degree — and turned into a parallel track I took seriously enough to build up a dozen industry certifications alongside my clinical training. My focus today is cloud security.

I like understanding cloud environments well enough to break them — an overly permissive IAM policy, an exposed storage bucket, a misconfigured security group that never should have shipped — and then using that same understanding to help teams lock them down. Medicine trained me to work a problem down to its root cause methodically, without skipping steps; that habit shows up in how I approach a cloud assessment just as much as it does in how I approach a diagnosis.
